# Welcome!

Welcome to my site! I am still updating it with more info like my resume, publications, and side projects (as of 9/25/26).

My name is Rachael Little and I am a Phd Candidate at the University of New Hampshire studying software obfuscation and binary analysis.

Specifically, my research areas focus on Mixed Boolean Arithmetic Obfuscation and Virtualization Obfuscation. Recently, I had the incredible experience of interning at Sandia National Labs, where I performed binary analysis on C and Rust binaries with embedded ML models.
